# Measurement Contract

This repository tests cross-domain generalization. Poker-derived labels are not assumed to transfer automatically.

## Candidate constructs

- **Pressure candidate:** observable escalation and commitment in public bidding.
- **Control candidate:** history-conditioned adjustment to opponent bidding patterns.
- **Chaos candidate:** strategically viable unpredictability after controlling for performance collapse.

All three are **candidates only** in v0.1.

## Prohibited shortcuts

- Do not define success by same-name policy/metric matching alone.
- Do not tune thresholds after confirmatory seeds.
- Do not treat randomness as Chaos without a performance floor.
- Do not treat predictability as Control without specificity tests.
